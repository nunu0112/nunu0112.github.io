Methods & Tools

Every method below is listed with the project where I actually used it, and with a link to the software involved. Sample code is at the bottom.

Cognitive Modeling

Reinforcement learning models of the Iowa Gambling Task — delta-rule value updating, outcome-representation learning, prospect valence learning. Sequential sampling models — the diffusion decision model, the racing diffusion model, the linear ballistic accumulator. Joint reinforcement learning–sequential sampling models, including derivation of the joint likelihood of choice and response time, and construction of the linking function between learned value and drift rate. Neural diffusion decision models with factor-analytic structure.

Racing diffusion model Diffusion decision model Outcome-representation learning Joint choice–RT modeling Factor-analytic linking functions

Applied in the IGT reinforcement learning–sequential sampling model and the lasso factor-analytic neural diffusion decision model.

Bayesian Estimation

Custom likelihood functions written directly in Stan, including the joint density of choice and response time and a first-passage-time density for the neural model. Weakly informative and shrinkage (Bayesian lasso) priors. Reparameterization to enforce bounded parameters without rejection sampling. Convergence diagnostics — R̂, effective sample size, divergent transitions, trace inspection. Posterior predictive checking by full generative simulation of the task, and simulation-based parameter recovery. Two-stage initialization strategies and SLURM array jobs for models that do not sample well from naive starting values.

Stan RStan hBayesDM Hamiltonian Monte Carlo / NUTS Parameter recovery Posterior predictive checks

Applied in the IGT joint model (seven individual-level parameters per participant) and the neural diffusion decision model. hBayesDM used to fit standard reinforcement learning and diffusion models as benchmarks for custom specifications.

Machine Learning & Interpretability

Tree-based ensembles with cross-validated evaluation; grouped and stratified resampling schemes for clustered data; class-imbalance handling by threshold adjustment, synthetic oversampling of mixed categorical–continuous features, and class-balanced bootstrapping; cluster bootstrap inference for model comparison. Shapley additive explanation values computed out-of-fold, with dummy-column contributions grouped back to their parent variables.

scikit-learn SHAP imbalanced-learn Random forests Grouped cross-validation Cluster bootstrap

Applied in the dyadic fertility-desire prediction project.

Causal Inference

Conditional average treatment effect estimation with generalized random forests: Robinson orthogonalization with forest-estimated nuisance functions, cross-fitted honest estimation, adaptive-neighborhood weighting, and doubly robust AIPW summaries. Formal calibration testing of both the average effect and the heterogeneity before any subgroup interpretation, with variable importance, best linear projection, and high/low-CATE group comparison used as triangulating evidence.

grf (R) Causal forests AIPW / doubly robust estimation Calibration testing

Applied in the COVID-19 well-being heterogeneous treatment effect project.

Experiment Programming & Data Collection

Design and implementation of response-time-sensitive tasks in Python, including control of motor confounds across items, deployed in live data collection; management of recruitment, experimental sessions, and compensation records.

PsychoPy Response time methodology

Applied as lab manager at the Psychometric & Computational Modeling Lab — see experience.

Neuroimaging & Structural Equation Modeling

Single-trial beta estimation by Least Squares Separate and ROI-level summarization within an atlas parcellation, on the analysis side of a joint neural–behavioral model. Preprocessing pipelines were practiced in graduate coursework; in research, neural data enter my work after preprocessing by collaborators. Structural equation modeling syntax covered in graduate coursework.
