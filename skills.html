Methods & Tools

Each method below is listed with the project in which it was applied.

Cognitive Modeling

Reinforcement learning models of the Iowa Gambling Task (delta-rule value updating, outcome-representation learning, prospect valence learning); sequential sampling models (the diffusion decision model, the racing diffusion model, the linear ballistic accumulator); joint reinforcement learning–sequential sampling models, including the joint likelihood of choice and response time and the linking function between learned value and drift rate; and neural diffusion decision models with factor-analytic structure. Applied in the Iowa Gambling Task joint model and the lasso factor-analytic neural diffusion decision model.

Bayesian Estimation

Custom likelihood functions written in Stan, including the joint density of choice and response time; weakly informative and shrinkage (Bayesian lasso) priors; reparameterization to enforce bounded parameters; convergence diagnostics (R̂, effective sample size, divergent transitions, trace inspection); posterior predictive checking by generative simulation of the task; and simulation-based parameter recovery. Applied in the two modeling projects above; hBayesDM used to fit standard reinforcement learning and diffusion models as benchmarks.

Stan RStan hBayesDM

Machine Learning and Interpretability

Tree-based ensembles with cross-validated evaluation; grouped and stratified resampling for clustered data; class-imbalance handling by threshold adjustment, synthetic oversampling of mixed categorical–continuous features, and class-balanced bootstrapping; cluster bootstrap inference for model comparison; and Shapley additive explanation values computed out-of-fold, with dummy-column contributions grouped back to their parent variables. Applied in the dyadic fertility-desire prediction project.

scikit-learn SHAP imbalanced-learn

Causal Inference

Conditional average treatment effect estimation with generalized random forests: Robinson orthogonalization with forest-estimated nuisance functions, cross-fitted honest estimation, adaptive-neighborhood weighting, and doubly robust AIPW summaries, with calibration testing of the average effect and of heterogeneity preceding any subgroup interpretation. Applied in the COVID-19 well-being project.

grf (R)

Software and Experimental Methods

Design and implementation of response-time-sensitive tasks in PsychoPy, including control of motor confounds across items, deployed in live data collection. Single-trial beta estimation and ROI-level summarization within an atlas parcellation on the analysis side of the joint neural–behavioral project; fMRI preprocessing in SPM and structural equation modeling in Mplus at coursework level. Programming in R, Stan, Python, and MATLAB, with formal training in computer science (B.S., Seoul National University).
